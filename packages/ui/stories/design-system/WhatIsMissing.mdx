### E03-S03 mentee cancellation: decisions still needed

The product rule is clear: a mentee cancellation at any time forfeits the payment, there is no refund (R27/D39), and the mentor receives the payment minus the operator-set fee after the payout hold (R34/D47). The screen example states those consequences without calculating a fee or payout date. It does not make a real request.

The available backlog, product brief, decisions and component documentation do not define:

- which booking/payment states permit mentee cancellation, and what happens if the mentor or operator has already changed the booking;
- whether a confirmed cancellation can be reversed or a booking restored;
- the cancellation endpoint, request/response schema, authorization/error codes, or idempotency key and duplicate-request behavior;
- what to display when the request times out or the connection is lost and the server outcome is unknown, how to reconcile status, and when retry is safe;
- whether confirmation requires a reason, additional acknowledgement or other input;
- the payout-hold duration/condition or how and when either party is notified;
- the post-confirmation route, treatment of session history, and whether a mentee can still open the session or contact the mentor.

Resolve these in the E03-S03 product/API specification and server contract before wiring the screen. The busy, error and success examples show a presentation boundary only; they do not settle those behaviors. (R27/R34, D39/D47; backlog E03-S03; Patterns/Delete confirmation.)
