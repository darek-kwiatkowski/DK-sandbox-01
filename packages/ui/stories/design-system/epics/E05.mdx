# E05 — Payouts

## Flow

The mentor completes Connect onboarding. A delivered session becomes payable after the platform fee; the operator can hold a payout. Product status labels G5 are approved, but provider mappings and the Connect screen contract are not. (E05-S01; R05, R28, R35; G5.)
