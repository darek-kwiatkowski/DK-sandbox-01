# E03 — Finding and booking

## Flow

Filter mentors by stack, inspect the profile and prices, choose a slot at least two hours ahead, review price and no-refund terms, then pay and receive server-confirmed booking status. The UI examples do not make a payment or reserve a slot. (E03-S01/S02/S03; R13, R14, R27, R29, R33.)

## Remaining product work

The backlog marks E03-S01/S02/S03 as not adopted. Real payments require founder-approved terms (R33) and an operator-set fee (R28); payment adapter, webhook reconciliation, notifications, atomic slot reservation and cancellation effects need their approved contracts. The current checkout text is a proposal and must not be used as final legal terms. (E03-S02-T01/T02; R27/R28/R32/R33; D46.)

The mentee cancellation stories are also presentation-only. The definitive no-refund and payout consequences are R27/R34 (D39/D47); booking eligibility, cancellation API, idempotency, unknown-result recovery, acknowledgement, payout-hold rules, notifications and post-cancellation navigation still need an E03-S03 contract. See [What is missing](?path=/docs/design-system-what-is-missing--docs).
