# Content

Documentation is in English; product UI examples remain in Polish, as approved for stage 0. Copy should be short, factual and useful. (Approved Q1; references P17, R01.5, A06.)

## Voice

The product is direct, calm and specific. It explains what happened and what the person can do next. It does not borrow urgency, social proof or course language. (P09, P17; A02, A03, A06; R14.)

### The product says

- “Sesja tekstowa z Anną Kowalską.” — name the task and the person. (R03, R20; R02.6.)
- “Sprawdź połączenie i spróbuj ponownie.” — state the next useful action after a network failure. (P17; WCAG 3.3.1.)
- “Termin: 20 listopada 2026, 18:00.” — present a slot as a date and time, not a speed promise. (R01.5, R14; K2.)

### The product does not say

- “Mentor odpowie w 5 minut!” — it promises speed the product does not guarantee. (R14; A06.)
- “Anna ma ocenę 4.9 od 200 osób.” — ratings and invented review counts are excluded. (N02; A03.)
- “Rozpocznij swoją ścieżkę mentoringową!” — the product is for a concrete answer, not a course journey. (A02; research summary in references.)

These forbidden examples are content anti-patterns, not factual claims about DevMentor or its mentors. (A02, A03, A06.)

## Buttons and links

- Start action labels with a verb and name the object when it clarifies the result: “Zapisz ceny”, “Opublikuj profil”, “Usuń profil”. Avoid “OK”, “Submit” and labels that omit the consequence. (User design-system brief: Content; R01.5.)
- Use a link label that describes its destination. For a new tab, announce that behavior in the accessible name or adjacent text. (WCAG 2.4.4; PublicWorkLink manual.)
- Keep labels concise, but let descriptive names wrap. Do not truncate essential names or require a tooltip to understand an action. (WCAG 1.4.10, 2.5.8; R01.1.)

## Errors and empty states

- Say what failed and give the next step: “Nie udało się zapisać cen. Sprawdź aktualne limity operatora.” (WCAG 3.3.1, 3.3.3; R29.)
- A field error names the field problem, stays associated with that field and does not clear other entered values. (WCAG 3.3.1; P18.)
- An empty list explains why it is empty and what to do next. A filter with no matches offers “Wyczyść filtry”; a list that has never had items offers its create action. (Approved stage-3 filtered-list pattern.)
- Never place an error requiring action in a toast, and never put necessary instructions only in a tooltip. (User design-system brief: Content and What to avoid; WCAG 3.3.1, 1.4.13.)

## Dates, time, money and numbers

- Format money in Polish locale and PLN, with two fractional digits: `120,00 zł`. The persistence contract is integer grosze; display with `formatPln(minor)` where the product helper is available. (P7; platform primitives P7/P16; R29.)
- Show the date and time with a timezone label whenever a slot crosses timezones. Keep the displayed zone explicit, for example `Europe/Warsaw`. (R14; platform primitives P16; WCAG 3.3.2.)
- Use the product helper `formatDateTime(iso, { timeZone })` rather than hand-building timezone-sensitive strings. (Platform primitives P16.)
- Do not shorten price to “from …” or show a countdown, viewer count or response-time estimate. (A06; R14.)

If a locale-specific format is not covered by the brief or an existing formatter, record it as a proposal before adding it. (User design-system brief: source rule.)
