# D45 — When a mentor cancels, the mentee loses the payment and the platform keeps it
- Date, owner: 2026-10-26, founder A (Oliwia) with founder B (Maciej)
- Context and the options weighed: Q31: R27 makes every cancellation forfeit the fee, while R30's replacement session covers only disputes and mentor no-shows.
