# D43 — A quality dispute or mentor no-show can be resolved with a free replacement session; the payout moves to the replacement mentor
- Date, owner: 2026-10-26, founder A (Oliwia) with founder B (Maciej)
- Context and the options weighed: With no refunds (D39), Q30 asked what a dispute resolution can do. Options: record the dispute and warn or remove the mentor; a free replacement session; refunds in disputes only. For the payout: move it to the replacement mentor; operator decides per case; platform pays the replacement mentor.
- Decision and why: The operator can grant the mentee a replacement session, with the same or another mentor, without a new payment; no money goes back to the mentee. The original mentor is not paid; the mentor who delivers the replacement receives the payout from the original payment.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Payouts must wait until a session is delivered or a dispute window has passed; how long that window is belongs in the spec.
- Confirmation: Confirmed in the discovery session on 2026-10-26 by founder B (Maciej), who reported that founder A (Oliwia) agrees; founder A's own confirmation was not observed in the session and she can supersede it.
- Status: active
