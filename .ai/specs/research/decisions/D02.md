# D02 — The developer pays for the first test

- Date, owner: 2026-10-24, Product owner
- Context and options weighed: The first test could be paid by the developer, an employer, or the developer's client. The Product owner chose personal payment by the developer.
- Decision and why: The developer pays for the first test. This is the selected test model, not evidence that developers will complete a purchase.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Observe an actual payment before building an employer or client purchase flow. Revisit if the test shows the intended developer cannot or will not pay personally.
- Status: active
- Required path to change: A superseding decision approved by the Product owner.
- Supersedes: none
