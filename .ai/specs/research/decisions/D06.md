# D06 — Run a manual, unpaid text-help experiment before the paid test

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Owner:** Product owner
- **Status:** active
- **Decision:** First test a release-blocking problem through a manually coordinated, unpaid text-help session. If the invited mentor explicitly declines or does not respond within 24 hours, record refusal. Keep the experiment within 25 work hours. Price and prepayment are for a later stage; price will be configurable then.
- **Why:** The product owner chose to first learn whether a mentor will participate and deliver text help for the selected situation before introducing payment.
- **Consequences:** This experiment does not test willingness to pay. The developer may share code only after removing sensitive data and access to internal systems; the developer is responsible for this and the system will not automatically inspect or police shared material. Session duration for this experiment is not set here; D04 applies to a later paid test.
- **Review by:** After the experiment.
- **Required path to change:** Superseding decision approved by the Product owner.
- **Supersedes:** D05
- **Source:** Product owner confirmation in discovery conversation on 2026-10-25.
