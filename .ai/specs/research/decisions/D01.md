# D01 — Focus the current MVP on paid text help

- Date, owner: 2026-10-24, Product owner
- Context and options weighed: The discovery brief is for the smallest MVP. The founder boards also describe a mentor marketplace, reusable knowledge vault, subscriptions, and MCP access. Those broader ideas remain future possibilities; this record sets the current discovery focus.
- Decision and why: Focus the current MVP on paid text help, as directed by the Product owner. This is a scope choice, not evidence that customers will pay.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Validate the user situation, purchase, and mentor delivery before planning a self-service product. Revisit if a real paid test contradicts this direction or the Product owner changes the scope.
- Status: active
- Required path to change: A superseding decision approved by the Product owner.
- Supersedes: none
