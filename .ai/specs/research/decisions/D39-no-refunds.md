# D39 — No refunds in 1.0; payment is final and any cancellation forfeits the fee
- Date, owner: 2026-10-26, founder A (Oliwia) with founder B (Maciej)
- Context and the options weighed: Q26 asked whether a free cancellation more than 24 hours ahead returns the payment and what happens on a no-show. Options: no refunds at all; refund only when cancelled more than 24 hours ahead; keep "no automatic refunds" with manual operator refunds.
- Decision and why: No refunds at all: payment is final once made; any cancellation, at any time, forfeits the fee. This also covers a mentor no-show. No reason was given beyond the choice.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: Replaces D10's free cancellation up to 24 hours before. What a quality dispute can resolve without a refund is open (Q30). The freelancer interview asked for a same-day refund on a no-show; the user terms (Q29) must state the policy.
- Confirmation: Confirmed in the discovery session on 2026-10-26 by founder B (Maciej), who reported that founder A (Oliwia) agrees; founder A's own confirmation was not observed in the session and she can supersede it.
- Status: active; supersedes D10 (cancellation and refund terms)
